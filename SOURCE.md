# Photo sources

Photos used on this den pitch. Saved under `public/photos/`.
Whitewater Excitement remains the copyright holder of their marketing stills.
Those are used here as public marketing photographs. The Angel Island and
Pinnacles frames are separately licensed, and credited below.

The original colour action set attached to the build request (scout group,
family boats, splashy South Fork runs, canvas camp) matches Whitewater
Excitement’s public library. Those frames were pulled from the live site
because the attachment copies were not on disk in this workspace.

## Current page photos

The pitch is three den weekends, not a den raft trip. The hero is the
Angel Island aerial. Pinnacles uses the Bear Gulch trail still. The Coloma
section reuses the outfitter's canvas-camp photo. The private-family raft
box, and only that box, uses one outfitter action frame, captioned as a
family trip rather than a den activity.

Files were resized for the page (Angel Island to 2000×1124, Bear Gulch to
1800×1350). Originals are linked below. Do not crop them further.
